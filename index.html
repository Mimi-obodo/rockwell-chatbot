About Rockwell Site Surveys

Rockwell Site Surveys is an engineering and site-survey company operating across Ireland and the United Kingdom. We provide comprehensive geotechnical assessments, environmental surveys, and structural inspections for residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects.

With coverage across 15 regions — from Dublin and Cork to Donegal and Kerry — our team combines deep local knowledge with cutting-edge technology to deliver accurate, timely survey data that helps projects move forward with confidence.
